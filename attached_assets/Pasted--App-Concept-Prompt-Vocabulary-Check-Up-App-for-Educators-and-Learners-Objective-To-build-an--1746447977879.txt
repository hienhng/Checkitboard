📚 App Concept Prompt: Vocabulary Check-Up App for Educators and Learners

🧠 Objective
To build an intuitive web/mobile app that simplifies the process of teaching, checking, and reinforcing vocabulary based on reading passages. The app is designed to empower educators with tools to monitor students’ vocabulary understanding, while providing students with interactive learning and review experiences.

🖥️ Welcome & Onboarding Flow
Upon opening the app, users are welcomed with a clean, minimal welcome screen.

Users must choose their role: Educator or Learner during sign-in.

🎓 Educator Experience
After logging in, educators:

Input a reading passage (via text box or upload).

Can highlight any word in the passage. Each highlighted word gets automatically added to the Daily Vocabulary Check-Up List.

After finalizing the selection, the educator can publish the vocabulary list to the Dashboard.

The published list becomes accessible to all assigned learners.

Post-submission actions:

Educators can view each student’s answers, assess them, and grade vocabulary comprehension.

For each incorrect word, the educator can mark it wrong, and the system will automatically add it to a reviewable flashcard list for that student.

🧑‍🎓 Learner Experience
After logging in, learners:

Can view the available Vocabulary Check-Up Lists on their Dashboard.

Upon clicking a list, they enter a split-screen interface:

Left side: The reading passage.

Right side: A vocab check-up test, displaying each word one-by-one.

For each word, learners write what they believe the word means.

After completing all words, they submit their answers.

If a word is marked wrong:

It is added to their personal flashcard review list.

They can later review and study these flashcards for future check-ups.

👪 Parent Integration
After grading, the system automatically sends a summary of the child’s performance to their parent’s email, including:

Overall score

List of incorrectly defined words

Encouragement tips or reminders for review

📦 Core Features
Role-based dashboard: Educator / Learner

Passage input + vocab selection via highlight

Auto-generated vocab list and daily check-up

Grading interface for educators

Flashcard system for reinforcement

Email report system for parents

Responsive UI (web/tablet/mobile)

🎯 Goals
Save educators’ time in vocabulary creation and assessment

Promote active recall and retention for learners

Keep parents informed and involved in vocabulary development

Use tech to personalize and adapt vocabulary learning journeys

📚 App Concept Prompt: Vocabulary Check-Up App for Educators and Learners

🧠 Objective
To create a streamlined web/mobile application that helps educators efficiently assess and reinforce vocabulary comprehension through reading passages. The app enables learners to engage actively with words, while giving educators and parents insight into each student’s vocabulary progress.

🔐 Login & User Roles
All users must log in using their email and a secure password.

Upon first login, users select their role as either:

Educator

Learner (Student)

🎓 Educator Flow
Once logged in as an educator, the user can:

Create a new reading activity by pasting or typing a passage.

Highlight specific words in the passage to add them to a Vocabulary Check-Up List.

After selecting the words, the educator can finalize and publish the list.

The published list appears on students' dashboards.

After students submit their check-ups:

Educators can view individual student responses, then grade each one.

Any incorrectly defined word can be marked, triggering it to be added automatically to that student’s flashcard review list.

🧑‍🎓 Learner Flow
After logging in via email as a learner:

Students see available Vocabulary Check-Up Lists on their dashboard.

Clicking a list opens a split-screen interface:

Left panel: The full reading passage.

Right panel: The check-up test, showing the highlighted words one at a time.

For each word, students type in what they believe the word means.

After finishing all words, they click Submit.

Once submitted:

The list is sent to the educator for grading.

Any word marked incorrect is automatically added to the student’s flashcard review list.

📩 Parent Integration
After grading, an email summary is sent to the parent (linked to the student’s account), including:

The student’s score

A list of incorrect words

Suggestions for improvement and study reminders

🧠 Flashcard System
Words marked incorrect during a check-up are stored in a personal flashcard list.

Students can access their flashcards anytime to review vocabulary.

Each flashcard includes:

The word

Student’s past answer

Correct definition

Option to mark as “learned”

⚙️ Core Features
Email-based login system

Role-based dashboards (Educator / Learner)

Passage input + interactive word selection

Automated vocab list creation

Daily vocab check-up interface

Educator grading panel

Flashcard generation for incorrect answers

Parent report emails

🎯 Goals
Make vocabulary assessment intuitive and efficient for educators

Encourage active learning and retention for students

Keep parents in the loop about their child’s vocabulary growth

Use simple tools to create a meaningful feedback and review cycle
